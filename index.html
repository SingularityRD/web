FAQ

What is the difference between AutoSecOps and AIDR?

AutoSecOps protects your company: it investigates security alarms and stops attacks, like an AI security team. AIDR protects your AI agents: it stops them from being tricked into doing harm. You can buy either one, or both.

Do I need both?

No. Each product works on its own. Together, anything AIDR blocks becomes a case that AutoSecOps investigates automatically.

How is AutoSecOps different from a traditional SOAR?

A traditional SOAR runs the scripts you write, and stops when reality doesn’t match them. AutoSecOps runs predictable workflows for known threats and AI agents that reason through new ones. Risky actions always wait for a person.

What makes AIDR different from a prompt firewall?

A prompt firewall only reads text. AIDR also watches what the agent does at the operating-system kernel: the programs it starts, the files it touches and the connections it opens. An agent can be tricked into hiding its intent in words; it can’t hide a system call.

Does it work with our existing tools?

Yes. AutoSecOps connects to 300+ tools, including CrowdStrike, SentinelOne, Microsoft Sentinel, Splunk and Palo Alto. If you don’t have them, our own EDR, NDR and SIEM cover the gap.

How do we stay in control of what the AI does?

You decide which actions run automatically, which need approval and who can approve them. Every step is recorded with its evidence, so any decision can be explained later.

Can it run on-premises or air-gapped?

Yes. It runs in our cloud, in your datacenter or fully air-gapped, with the Maximus 1B model on your own hardware. Sensitive data never has to leave.
