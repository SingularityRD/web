Questions buyers ask

Questions about the models

What are the Singularity Foundation Models, exactly?

The Singularity Foundation Models are AI models Singularity trained from scratch, not a wrapper around another vendor's API. The family has three members: Singularity Cyber for security work, Singularity General for documents, analysis and code, and Frontier Cyber for approved government and defense institutions.

Can they really run fully air‑gapped, inside our own datacenter?

Yes. The Singularity Foundation Models run on hardware you own, in your cloud, your datacenter or a fully air-gapped site with no internet connection. They are sized to fit in your own server racks, and we size the hardware with your team.

Who controls the model once it is installed?

You do. The Singularity Foundation Models run in your building, retain no data, and cannot be shut off remotely by anyone. The weights are installed and auditable on site. Frontier Cyber adds a contract, an approval process and a full audit trail.

How good is it next to the big cloud models?

By Singularity's internal measurement, the flagship scores 88.0 on CyberGym, ahead of published frontier results. On general intelligence Singularity estimates 44 on the Artificial Analysis Index v5 scale: ahead of Claude Sonnet 5.5 (31) and GPT-6.1 Terra (38), behind GPT-6 Astra (52) and Claude Opus 5.5 (48).

Why not sign a contract with a cloud AI provider instead?

A cloud provider's model runs on its servers, so your data leaves the building, and access can be cut by that provider or by another government's rules. The Singularity Foundation Models run on your hardware, where nobody outside can switch them off.
