Why AutoSecOps

Six reasons to choose AutoSecOps

01 · Coverage

No alert waits until morning

Your team cannot read thousands of alerts a day, and night alerts sit until someone logs in. AutoSecOps triages every alert as it arrives, gathers the evidence and writes a verdict. Your analysts start with a case, not a queue.

  • Every alert triaged: SIEM, EDR, email, identity, cloud
  • Duplicates removed, indicators of compromise extracted and enriched
  • Verdict, severity and written summary inside the case
  • Case copilot and workspace chat beside every case
  • Skill packs for hunting, detection rules and incident response

02 · Human approval

The AI proposes. Your people make the call.

Nobody wants software disabling accounts on its own. AutoSecOps runs lookups and enrichment without waiting, and holds anything serious, such as disabling an account or isolating a laptop, until a person says yes. You set which actions need approval.

  • Read-only lookups and enrichment run without waiting
  • See each action's arguments, then approve, edit or reject
  • One approval inbox, plus approval buttons in Slack
  • Recommendation-only mode before any automation goes live
  • Watchtower shows what coding agents call over MCP

03 · AI safety

A hidden instruction in an alert cannot steer it

In June 2025 a single email hijacked an AI assistant (EchoLeak). Attackers now hide orders to the AI inside their emails. AutoSecOps treats those as part of the attack: its agents use only the tools you allow, and any change still goes to a person.

  • Agents can call only the tools you allow
  • Isolated sandbox with no internet access by default
  • AI keys stay outside the sandbox; token budget per agent
  • Mapped to OWASP Top 10, LLM and Agentic Top 10

04 · Speed

New tools and playbooks in minutes, not weeks

Connecting a new tool used to mean API documents, code and testing. A new playbook, a written response plan, needed a developer. With AutoSecOps you pick an integration and enter a key, then draw the playbook or describe it in plain words.

  • 2,000+ integrations across SIEM, EDR, identity, cloud and chat
  • Drag-and-drop builder, or describe the playbook in chat
  • Runs survive restarts; rerun from any step
  • Locked versions, checks before publishing, one-step rollback
  • Your own Python actions, synced from GitHub, GitLab or Bitbucket

05 · Audit

When the auditor asks, the answer is already written

KVKK (Turkish data protection law), BDDK (the Turkish banking regulator) and Cybersecurity Law No. 7545 want proof of response, and so do DORA and NIS2 in the EU. AutoSecOps records who proposed, who approved and when, in the case itself.

  • Proposing agent, approver and timestamp in every case
  • Every action in the audit log, with audit webhooks
  • SLA tracking, duration metrics and reports for management
  • Single sign-on, SCIM, custom roles and IP allowlists
  • Encrypted secrets with masking and external vaults

06 · Your infrastructure

Your alerts and prompts can stay in your building

Many banks and public bodies cannot send logs to someone else's cloud. Deploy AutoSecOps on-premises with a local AI model, and no logs, events or prompts leave your network. Choose a cloud AI provider instead, and prompts go to that provider.

  • One server in minutes, or Kubernetes via Helm
  • Fully air-gapped, with the license checked offline
  • Local models: Singularity Foundation Models, Ollama, vLLM, LiteLLM
  • Tunnels reach internal systems with short-lived access
  • First-party EDR, NDR and SIEM sensors if you have none

Questions buyers ask

Questions about AutoSecOps

What exactly is AutoSecOps?

AutoSecOps is an AI security operations platform from Singularity. AI analysts investigate every security alert around the clock, filter out false alarms and open a case for each real threat. They respond through 2,000+ integrations with your existing tools and ask a person before any critical step.

Can we run it on-premises, or fully air‑gapped?

Yes. AutoSecOps runs in a managed cloud, on your own servers or fully air-gapped, with no internet connection and the license checked offline. With on-premises deployment and a local model, such as the Singularity Foundation Models, no logs, events or prompts leave your network.

If the AI wants to disable an account, who has the final say?

Your team does. AutoSecOps runs read-only lookups on its own, but disabling accounts or isolating devices waits for approval in its inbox or in Slack. The approver sees the exact arguments and can approve, edit or reject. Agent, approver and time are recorded in the case.

Can an attacker hide instructions in an alert to steer the AI?

AutoSecOps is built for that attack. Its agents can call only the tools you allow, run in a sandbox with no internet access, and send any change to a person. In a product demo, an email ordering the AI to delete its case failed; the case stayed open.

Do we have to replace our SIEM and EDR?

No. AutoSecOps works alongside the security tools you already own, reading from them and acting through 2,000+ integrations. Your tools keep raising alerts; AutoSecOps investigates them. If you have no tools yet, Singularity's own EDR, NDR and SIEM sensors can cover the gap.
