The problem

Failures are inevitable. Chaos isn’t.

01

Finding the cause is slow

Finding the cause often takes longer than fixing it. At 3 a.m. the on-call engineer chases it across several screens.

02

Knowledge sits with a few

The fix lives in the heads of a few experienced staff. What happens when they are on leave?

03

Nobody wrote it down

Who did what, and why? Often it is recorded nowhere, so management and auditors get no clear answer.

How it works

Four steps. The same routine every time.

  1. 1

    Detects

    Groups scattered alerts from your monitoring tools into one incident, so it is clear which service is hit and who owns it.

  2. 2

    Investigates

    Finds the cause and shows the evidence. It also says plainly when it is not sure.

  3. 3

    Asks

    Proposes a fix from a list you approved and, when needed, sends it to the owner for approval.

  4. 4

    Resolves and verifies

    Applies the fix and measures the result. If it does not work, it undoes the change and alerts a person.

Questions about IT Ops

What is Singularity IT Ops?

IT Ops is an AI on-call engineer from Singularity. It groups alerts from your monitoring tools into one incident, finds the cause with evidence, proposes a fix, applies it with your approval, checks the result and records every step.

Who is IT Ops for?

IT Ops is for companies whose IT teams are on call for outages. IT managers get a less exhausted team, security and compliance teams get every step logged, and executives get shorter outages with AI kept under control.

How is IT Ops different from a monitoring tool?

A monitoring tool tells you something is wrong. IT Ops reads the alerts from the tools you already have, finds the cause, proposes a fix, applies it with approval and keeps the record. Your existing tools stay in place.

Does IT Ops run on-premises or air-gapped?

Yes. IT Ops is installed in your own environment and works in air-gapped networks, which have no internet connection. You choose the AI provider, and it can run on the Singularity Foundation Models on your own hardware.

How do people stay in control of IT Ops?

IT Ops cannot write its own commands. It picks fixes from a list you approved, on systems you listed. High-impact fixes always wait for a person’s approval, nobody can approve their own request, and one button stops every automated action.
