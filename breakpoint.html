How it works

Find. Prove. Fix. Re-test.

  1. 1

    Find

    Breakpoint probes your code, web apps, APIs and AI features the way a real attacker would.

  2. 2

    Prove

    A finding counts only when it has been reproduced, with the evidence attached. No guesswork.

  3. 3

    Fix

    Each proven finding comes with a fix that is ready for your team to review.

  4. 4

    Re-test

    The same attack is run again after the fix. The finding is closed only when the attack fails.

Questions about Breakpoint

What is Singularity Breakpoint?

Breakpoint is an AI pentester from Singularity. It attacks your code, web apps, APIs and AI apps every day, the way a real attacker would, proves each finding by reproducing it and delivers a fix your team can review.

Who is Breakpoint for?

Breakpoint is for teams that ship software often and must prove it is secure: banks and fintech, insurance and capital markets, SaaS and software companies, telecom, energy and utilities, the public sector and defense, and e-commerce and retail.

How is Breakpoint different from scanners and annual pentests?

A scanner raises suspicions and an annual pentest is a snapshot. Breakpoint attacks every day and counts a finding only when it has been reproduced. Keep the certified annual pentest where regulators require it; Breakpoint covers the time between tests.

Can Breakpoint run on-premises or air‑gapped?

Yes. Breakpoint runs in your own environment with the AI provider you choose. It can also run fully air-gapped, meaning offline with no outbound connection. Your code, findings and evidence stay on your machines.

How do people stay in control of Breakpoint?

Breakpoint tests only the targets you approve in writing, inside limits you set. Fixes are proposed for review and nothing is merged without your team. Every test run keeps a record of what it did.
