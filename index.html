01 · How it works

Every card payment is authorized first. No AI action is. Until now.

When you tap your card, the payment is checked before the money moves. Singularity does the same for AI agents: every tool call is checked before it runs — cleared, held for a human, or denied.

See it on your agents ›

01 · DECLARED Live

The agent asks first.

Every agent host now announces each action before it runs — the tool call, the task and the session. Singularity is the one who answers. Nothing to change inside the agent.

02 · CLEARED Live

Cleared, held or denied — in 0.35 s.

The action is judged with everything that came before it. Reading a secret is fine. Reading a secret and then sending it out is one chain — and it never runs.

03 · OBSERVED Rolling out

What ran is compared with what was declared.

A kernel sensor checks what really happened on the machine, so an agent can't say one thing and do another.

04 · PROVEN Rolling out

A signed record for every action.

"Who cleared this agent?" finally has an answer. Each action lands in a signed audit chain, exported to your SIEM and ready for the auditor.

Frequently asked questions

What does Singularity do, in one sentence?

Like a card network authorizing every payment, Singularity clears every AI agent action — each command, file access, tool call and MCP request — before it runs, and holds or denies the dangerous ones.

Which agents and AI tools do you cover?

Six agent hosts — Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, VS Code, OpenCode and OpenClaw — plus MCP through our gateway, and eight AI chat apps through Browser Guard. One engine, one policy, on unmodified hosts.

Will it slow my developers down?

The median decision takes 0.35 seconds on the endpoint. In our benchmark, zero everyday developer actions were hard-blocked and 1.6% were held for a human to approve.

Does any of our data leave our network?

No. Singularity runs on-prem, in your private cloud or fully air-gapped. Local models run on your hardware, there are no external LLM calls, and telemetry and evidence carry counts and digests, not content.

Is this only about attackers?

No. We also stop agents that go wrong on their own: runaway loops and budget overruns, reward hacking such as deleting tests to turn CI green, agents spawning agents, irreversible commands like terraform destroy, and attempts to switch us off.

What do auditors and regulators get?

A signed audit chain streamed to Splunk, QRadar, Sentinel or Elastic, an AI-BOM in CycloneDX 1.6, and evidence mapped to the EU AI Act, ISO 42001, NIST and KVKK. Every block is explained, and rules are readable and mapped to MITRE ATLAS.

How is it licensed?

An annual licence per deployment. One deployment covers every agent, host and environment you connect, so coverage grows without new contracts.
