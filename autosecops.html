Questions about AutoSecOps

What is AutoSecOps?

AutoSecOps is an AI security team from Singularity. Its AI analysts investigate every security alert around the clock, filter out false alarms and open a written case for each real threat. They stop the threat and ask your team before any serious step.

Who is AutoSecOps for?

AutoSecOps is for organizations whose security team gets more alerts than it can review, such as banks, factories, hospitals, defense and government bodies. IT, finance, revenue operations, people, legal and engineering teams can use the same workflows to automate their own work.

How is AutoSecOps different from a traditional SOAR?

A traditional SOAR, a tool that automates security response, runs the scripts you write and stops when reality does not match them. AutoSecOps runs predictable workflows for known threats and AI analysts that reason through new ones. Risky actions always wait for a person.

Can AutoSecOps run on-premises or air-gapped?

Yes. AutoSecOps runs in the cloud, in your own datacenter or fully air-gapped, meaning with no connection to the internet. It can run on the Singularity Foundation Models, on your own hardware, so sensitive data never has to leave.

How do people stay in control of AutoSecOps?

You decide which actions AutoSecOps runs on its own, which need approval and who can approve them. Serious steps, such as disabling an account, wait for a person. Every step is recorded with its evidence, so any decision can be explained later.
