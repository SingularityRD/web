Frequently asked questions

Does AutoSecOps work with our existing tools?

Yes. AutoSecOps connects to tools such as CrowdStrike, Splunk, Sentinel and Palo Alto. If you need them, you can also use our own sensors.

What does AIDR protect against?

AIDR checks prompts, retrieved content and tool calls before execution. It stops prompt injection, tool abuse and data exfiltration. Safe actions continue; risky actions are blocked or sent to a person for approval.

How long does deployment take?

You can connect an existing stack or deploy our sensors in minutes. Timing varies with environment, scope and approval requirements.

What is BreachOps?

BreachOps continuously tests your defenses the way a real adversary would — inside approved scope, with a rollback path — and ships every finding with evidence and a fix.

How much does AutoSecOps cost?

Starter is $8 per device per month, Growth is $12, and Enterprise is custom priced for regulated or air-gapped environments.

Can AutoSecOps run air-gapped or on-premises?

Yes. AutoSecOps can run in the cloud, in your datacenter, or fully air-gapped. Sensitive data can stay inside your environment.
