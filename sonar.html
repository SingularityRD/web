Questions about Sonar

What is Sonar?

Sonar is Singularity’s security product for factory and other industrial networks, often called OT (operational technology). It works like a security camera for the plant network: it listens, alerts on suspicious activity and keeps the evidence, without stopping production.

Who is Sonar for?

Sonar is for organizations that run industrial sites: manufacturing plants, energy and grid operators, oil, gas and water utilities, building and rail systems, and defense and public sector sites. It gives plant managers and security leaders one view of the plant network.

How is Sonar different from a firewall?

A firewall sees traffic pass but keeps no record of which device sent which command. Sonar records how machines talk to each other, flags unknown devices and unusual commands, and keeps the evidence. Sonar monitors and alerts; it does not block.

Can Sonar run on-premises or air‑gapped?

Yes. Sonar runs on your own server at the site and needs no internet connection, so it works air-gapped, meaning cut off from outside networks. Your data stays on site and nothing flows outward.

Can Sonar slow down or stop production?

No. Sonar only listens. It never sends a command to your machines, so it adds no load and no risk of downtime. Sonar also does not block anything: it raises an alert, and your team decides what to do.
