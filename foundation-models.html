Questions about the models

What are the Singularity Foundation Models?

The Singularity Foundation Models are AI models that Singularity trained from scratch, not a wrapper around another vendor’s API. The family has three members: Singularity Cyber, Singularity General and Frontier Cyber.

Can the Singularity Foundation Models run air‑gapped?

Yes. The Singularity Foundation Models run on your own hardware, in your cloud, your datacenter or a fully air-gapped site with no internet connection. We size the hardware with you. Requirements are in the technical brief.

How do the Singularity Foundation Models compare with other frontier models?

By Singularity’s internal measurement, our best model scores 88.0 on CyberGym, ahead of the results published for other frontier models. On general intelligence, Singularity estimates 44 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index v5 scale: ahead of Claude Sonnet 5.5 (31) and GPT-6.1 Terra (38) at medium reasoning effort, and behind GPT-6 Astra (52) and Claude Opus 5.5 (48).

Who can buy Frontier Cyber?

Frontier Cyber is the offense-grade member of the Singularity Foundation Models. Singularity sells it only to approved government and defense institutions, under contract and with a full audit trail.

Which Singularity products use the models?

Singularity’s AI products can run on the Singularity Foundation Models on your own hardware. Those products are AutoSecOps, AIDR, IT Ops and Breakpoint.
