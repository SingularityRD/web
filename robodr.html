RoboDR · Robot security

Stop a hijacked robot safely. Prove what happened.

RoboDR is a security layer for robots. It watches for attacks around the clock, brings the robot to a safe stop without a fall or collision, and keeps a tamper-proof record.

The problem

A robot is a computer with motors

01

Attacks become physical harm

A compromised laptop loses data. A hijacked robot can strike a person, a machine or the facility itself.

02

Most robots are unprotected

A vulnerability published in 2025 let attackers take control of widely used robots over Bluetooth alone. A single incident can halt a whole fleet.

03

You must show evidence

After an incident you must answer what happened and who intervened, with evidence. Regulators now want proof of cybersecurity in robots.

How it works

Three jobs: see, stop, prove

  1. 1

    See

    RoboDR watches the robot’s network, every command sent to its motors and its onboard computer, around the clock, and catches abnormal behavior.

  2. 2

    Stop

    It cuts off dangerous commands before they reach the robot and brings it to a safe stop, without a fall or collision.

  3. 3

    Prove

    Every incident is written to a tamper-proof black box, like a flight recorder, and your security team is alerted at once.

Features

Everything in RoboDR

Every capability, from reading motor commands to signed evidence, with people approving the actions that matter.

Motion and network

  • Decodes Unitree and ROS 2 motion, arm and hand commands
  • Flags unknown senders on the robot’s network, even under new identities
  • Spoofed, replayed or out-of-limit speed, joint and torque commands
  • Compares what the robot was told with what it did
  • Faked or frozen telemetry and disagreeing navigation sensors
  • Links events into attacks such as key theft or motion hijacking
  • Never reports “all clear” when its own inputs go silent

On the robot’s computer

  • New processes and outbound connections on the robot
  • Package installs and changes to ROS 2 and robot apps
  • Bluetooth and Wi-Fi setup tools and wireless setting changes
  • Reverse tunnels, VPNs, code injection and fileless execution
  • Reads of the robot’s private keys and unexpected kernel modules
  • Attempts to change or stop RoboDR itself
  • Published robot attacks, including UniPwn (CVE-2025-35027)

Safe response

  • Escalates in stages: alert, restrict, slow down, pause, safe state
  • Isolates the robot from the network or quarantines a rogue sender
  • Every motion command passed, limited or rejected
  • Slows the robot without changing its direction; stops never delayed
  • Never cuts motor power or resets the emergency stop

Your SOC, with AI

  • Each finding opens a case in AutoSecOps automatically
  • AI agent triages each finding and recommends a response
  • The AI can never delete cases, run scripts or go online
  • Attacker text from the robot flagged, never taken as instructions
  • Forged, stale or unsigned findings rejected and logged
  • Findings in open OCSF format for any compatible SIEM

Signed evidence

  • Black box records survive power loss; any deletion shows
  • Auditors can verify records but cannot forge them
  • Every finding signed, mapped to MITRE ATT&CK and tied to evidence

Questions about RoboDR

What is RoboDR?

RoboDR is a security layer for robots from Singularity. It sees an attack on a robot as it begins, brings the robot to a safe stop without a fall or collision, and keeps a tamper-proof record of what happened.

Who is RoboDR for, and which robots does it support?

RoboDR is for sectors where a robot cannot afford to fail: defense, energy, manufacturing, logistics, public safety and robot manufacturers. It supports Unitree quadruped and humanoid robots, and any robot running ROS 2, the open-source robot software, out of the box.

How is RoboDR different from traditional security software?

Traditional security software protects the computer. RoboDR protects the robot. It reads the commands sent to the motors and keeps humanoid and four-legged robots balanced during a safe stop. RoboDR is not a safety-rated component; it complements the robot’s own safety system.

Can RoboDR run on-premises or at air‑gapped sites?

Yes. RoboDR runs on your own servers or in your cloud. It also works at air-gapped sites, meaning sites with no internet connection, and no component ever calls out.

How do people stay in control of RoboDR?

RoboDR starts in monitor-only mode, and protection is switched on only when you are ready. Your rules limit dangerous commands, and pausing the robot or putting it in a safe state needs two people’s approval. Each incident and approval is kept in one signed record.
