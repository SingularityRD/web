Why AIDR

Six reasons to choose AIDR

01 · Agent attacks

A tricked agent never gets to act

Coding agents read READMEs, emails and web pages. One hidden instruction can turn an agent against you. AIDR checks every command, file write, web request and MCP call (an agent’s link to other systems) before it runs. The harmful step stops; normal work carries on.

  • Hidden instructions in READMEs, documents and emails
  • Poisoned tool output hidden before the AI reads it
  • MCP servers and tools that turn malicious after approval
  • Multi-step attacks judged as one chain
  • Key theft, fake packages and disguised destructive commands

02 · Agent mistakes

Irreversible actions wait for a person

No attacker is needed for harm. An agent chasing a passing test can delete the checks. One cleaning up can destroy your infrastructure. AIDR holds these actions for a person and pauses runaway sessions. Fixing code and running tests go straight through.

  • Deleting databases or cloud infrastructure needs approval
  • Publishing or messaging in your name needs approval
  • Deleted tests, disabled CI and force-pushes are held
  • Loops, error spikes and blown budgets pause the session
  • Agents launching agents with permission prompts off are blocked

03 · Customer data

Staff keep using AI while customer data stays inside

Banning AI sites does not work. People carry on from their phones and the risk goes out of sight. AIDR lets them keep working: names, ID numbers, IBANs and keys are masked in the browser, and the reply is restored only on their screen.

  • ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Copilot, Perplexity, DeepSeek, Mistral and Grok
  • Turkish ID numbers and IBANs checked for validity, not guessed
  • Masked copies of Office, PDF, CSV and ZIP files
  • Screenshots with personal data are held back (preview)
  • Project code names and your own customer records

04 · Control

A hijacked agent cannot switch off its guard

An agent hijacked by a hidden instruction will try to disable whatever stops it. With AIDR, approval lives outside the agent: only a person can give it, once, for a limited time. Attempts to turn AIDR off are blocked and logged.

  • Single-use, time-limited approvals, given only by a person
  • Attempts to disable or reconfigure AIDR are blocked
  • On an internal error, the call is denied
  • Every decision shows its rule and reasoning
  • Kernel-level check of what agents really run, on Linux (preview)

05 · Visibility

Know every agent and AI tool in use

Most teams cannot list which agents run, with which permissions, or which MCP servers they connect to. AIDR finds them, spots shadow AI (tools used without IT’s approval) and checks personal accounts on AI sites, so you govern what is really there.

  • Discovers agents and MCP servers across the company
  • Controls personal accounts on AI chat sites
  • Scans agent skills and MCP servers for known vulnerabilities
  • AI bill of materials (AI-BOM) listing your AI components

06 · Evidence

Audit answers are ready before the auditor asks

Turkey’s Law No. 7545, BDDK rules, KVKK, DORA, NIS2 and the EU AI Act all ask for control over AI. AIDR keeps a signed record of every decision and maps your rules to these frameworks clause by clause, with gaps shown.

  • Tamper-proof signed log; sensitive values are never written
  • One-click KVKK and GDPR report
  • Monthly governance scorecard for management
  • Incidents sent to Splunk, Sentinel, Elastic, QRadar or AutoSecOps
  • Signed receipt for each agent action, on Linux (preview)

Questions buyers ask

Questions about AIDR

What is AIDR, in plain words?

AIDR is Singularity’s security product for AI. It masks customer data before employees send it to AI chat sites, and it checks every action of coding agents such as Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode and OpenClaw, plus MCP tools, before it runs. Every decision is logged.

Can AIDR run on our own servers or an air‑gapped network?

Yes. AIDR runs on your own servers, management console included, or on air-gapped networks with a signed offline package. Decisions are made on your machines and no data is sent to Singularity. Protection stays on without a network, and even after the license expires.

Who has the final say when AIDR holds an action?

A person does. AIDR lets everyday work through, holds irreversible actions for approval and blocks harmful ones. Approvals are single-use, time-limited and given only by an authorized person, never by the agent. You set the rules, and each decision shows the rule behind it.

Will AIDR slow my developers down?

No. With AIDR, safe calls such as fixing code and running tests go straight through, and decisions are made on the device. Developers only notice AIDR when an action is risky. Cursor and Copilot in VS Code are supported in preview.

Why not just ban ChatGPT?

Bans do not work: blocked employees carry on from their phones, and the risk goes out of sight. AIDR lets staff keep using ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude and Copilot while names, Turkish ID numbers, IBANs and keys are masked before they leave the browser.
